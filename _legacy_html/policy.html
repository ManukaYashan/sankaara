7 POLICIES
ARTICLE I schedule

Check-in & Check-out Protocol

Check-in commences from 2:00 PM (14:00). Check-out is respectfully requested by 11:00 AM to allow our housekeeping staff sufficient unhurried time for continuous natural air circulation, deep allergen cleansing, and bespoke botanical linen preparation.

Early decelerations or late departures are subject to room turnover cycles. Guests are warmly invited to rest in the Lotus Library Pavilion with complete baggage handling.

Standard Protocol
ARTICLE II volume_off

Intimate Property & Acoustic Harmony

As an intimate 5-suite mountain retreat dedicated to restorative meditation and natural soundscapes, Silent Contemplation Hours take effect nightly at 10:00 PM (22:00) through 07:00 AM.

Audio media, streaming calls, and personal sound systems within open breezeways require headphones. We cherish the ambient murmur of mountain stream water and evening forest cicadas.

Acoustic Preservation
ARTICLE III credit_card

Cancellation & Prepayment Accord

Due to our boutique scale of only five suites, cancellations require mindful notice. Reservations may be altered or released with a full refund up to 48 hours prior to the scheduled 14:00 check-in time.

Releases within 48 hours or unnotified absences incur the first night tariff. Weather advisories impacting highlands transport are coordinated through concierge flexibility.

Flexible 48-Hour Threshold
ARTICLE IV groups

Children & Suite Occupancy

Each private sanctuary suite accommodates a maximum of two adults to maintain privacy, personal safety along stone cliffside paths, and contemplative balance throughout the estate.

S A N K Ā R A welcomes individuals aged 14 and older. For multi-generational family journeys or younger guests, private whole-sanctuary reservations are offered on inquiry.

Ages 14+ / 2 Guests per Suite
ARTICLE V nature_people

Smoking & Botanical Living

S A N K Ā R A is a 100% smoke-free sanctuary indoors. Smoking, vaping, and electronic vapor systems are prohibited within all suites, stone rain-showers, and dining terraces.

Designated gravel garden alcoves are positioned away from ventilation lines. In respect of the adjacent rainforest bio-reserve, unattended outdoor flames and loose incense are barred.

Pure Air Living
ARTICLE VI cruelty_free

Fauna Preservation & Pet Accord

To safeguard the sensitive Knuckles biosphere reserve, endemic songbird nesting clusters, and highland wildlife wandering the forest fringe, domestic pets cannot be hosted.

Certified service animals are welcomed with prior notice to coordinate wildlife boundary buffers and ensure specialized pavilion care.

Wildlife Sanctuary

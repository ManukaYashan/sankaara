SANCTUARY ARCHITECTURE

AN INTIMATE 5-ROOM RETREAT

Perched along a quiet high-altitude ridge, SANKĀRA was conceived as a silent counterweight to modern acceleration. Spanning four hectares of ancient Ceylon cloud-forest, the sanctuary limits residency to precisely five private suites, ensuring absolute auditory stillness and personal sovereignty.

Every boundary is sculpted from raw monolithic granite, warm reclaimed teak, and hand-troweled lime plaster. Here, hospitality is not transactional; each guest is matched with a dedicated private butler who curates unhurried rhythms, bespoke Ayurvedic dining, and unobtrusive presence.

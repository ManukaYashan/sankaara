JOURNEYS TO TRANQUILITY

HOW TO GET HERE

Whether arriving via helicopter charter directly onto our private ridge helipad or taking the historic colonial rail through cloud-veiled tea country, the transition is intentional and unhurried.

flight
01 / ACCESS OPTION

Commercial & Private Aviation

Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) is 3.5 hours by scenic road. Direct charter helicopter service lands directly at S A N K Ā R A Private Ridge Helipad (35 min flight from Colombo).

Helicopter transfers arranged through Sanctuary Concierge 72 hrs prior.
train
02 / ACCESS OPTION

Scenic Highland Rail & Drive

Take the historic colonial Viceroy Luxury Train to Kandy Station or Hatton. From Kandy, our private estate 4x4 collects guests for an immersive 1.5-hour climb through lush organic tea estates and pine forests.

Secure heated private parking and complimentary EV charging on estate grounds.
directions_car
03 / ACCESS OPTION

Door-to-Door Sanctuary Chauffeur

Relax in our climate-controlled Land Rover or Mercedes luxury fleet with Starlink Wi-Fi, organic chilled botanical refreshments, and estate-curated ambient audio.

Reserve private chauffeur during room reservation or via concierge.
